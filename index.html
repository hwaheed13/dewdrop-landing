ML bucket predictions with bet signals

A machine learning model trained on historical NWS, AccuWeather, and atmospheric data predicts today's and tomorrow's most likely 2-degree Kalshi bucket. Each prediction is labeled STRONG BET, LEAN, or SKIP based on model confidence.

How it works

  • 1
    Log every forecast update — NWS and AccuWeather forecasted highs are captured throughout the day, timestamped, and stored in a database. Over time this builds a bias profile for each source.
  • 2
    Fetch real-time atmospheric data — Solar irradiance, cloud cover, 850mb and 925mb temperatures, wind speed and direction are pulled from Open-Meteo every 15 minutes and included as live model features.
  • 3
    Run the ML model — A gradient boosting model retrained daily on all available historical data predicts the probability of each 2-degree Kalshi bucket. The top prediction gets a STRONG BET, LEAN, or SKIP label based on confidence and probability gap to the next bucket.
  • 4
    Score reversal risk — The Reversal Risk panel checks six signals (probability gap, model spread, observed temperature trajectory, 925mb temperature, wind direction, and agency divergence) and compares them against historical flip rates for similar setups.
  • 5
    Score and learn — After settlement, every prediction is scored WIN or MISS against the official daily high. Results are stored transparently. The model retrains each morning incorporating the previous day's outcome.
